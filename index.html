Free Paper Finder

Paste one or more DOIs, or any text that contains them. It lists every free, legal copy it can find, and names the author to ask when there isn't one.

"Accepted" is the author's peer-reviewed manuscript before the journal's layout. A preprint was posted before or during review, so check its numbers against the published version.
